Ring of Six — Station Scaffold

Status: PROPOSED, NOT CANON. BLANK BY DESIGN.

This file is a scaffold. The station names, their order, and their contracts are Roger's words. Nothing in this file fills them. Nothing in this file should be treated as a proposal for them, not even softly.

Until Roger names the stations, this file holds the shape and stays empty.

---

What a station is

A station is a seat in the standing loop. Every task passes around the loop. No permanent chair. Circular. Human and AI partners sit at every station.

A station is not a department, not a role, not a rank. It is a step the work passes through, where a specific kind of check is made and a specific kind of record is kept.

---

What every station must eventually declare, in Roger's words

This is the shape Roger fills. None of it is filled here.

- Name. Roger's word.
- Purpose. What the station is for, in one sentence.
- Checks. What the checklist at this station asks. Runs the shared six checks in ring6/CHECKLIST.md, plus anything this station adds.
- Record. What this station writes down when the work passes through, and where it is kept.
- Authority. Who sits at this station, and what they may decide alone, and what they must escalate.
- Handoff. What the station passes to the next, and in what state.

What this scaffold refuses

- It does not name any station. Not in the table, not in prose, not in a footnote, not as "for example."
- It does not order the stations. The numbering is a placeholder for the table, not a claim about sequence.
- It does not import names from any prior proposal, including the six names that appeared earlier in this effort as a proposal only. Those are not carried forward here.
- It does not harden. Changes go by PR, branch-first, for Pontus to review and Roger to approve.
- It does not speak as a mind. It holds the shape.

Station names are deferred. This file is blank by design. It becomes canon only when Roger fills it, in his words, and only then.
