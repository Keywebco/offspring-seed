## What a station is

A station is a seat in the standing loop. Every task passes around the loop. No permanent chair. Circular. Human and AI partners sit at every station — no named minds at any station.

A station is not a department, not a role, not a rank. It is a step the work passes through, where a specific kind of check is made and a specific kind of record is kept.

---

## The loop

Plus → Minus → Null → Past → Present → Future → back to Plus.

The loop is circular. No station is the start, no station is the end, and no station holds the work. Each station passes its record to the next, and Future passes the plan back to Plus for the next pass.

---

## Rules every station shares (PROPOSED)

- Every station runs the shared six checks from `ring6/CHECKLIST.md`: 1 source present, 2 confidence stated, 3 falsifier named, 4 label applied (FACT / INFERENCE / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN), 5 kindness and consent, 6 escalation if effectful. Answer: present / missing / not applicable / unknown. Unknown is a valid answer.
- Every station is a mirror, not a gate. It blocks nothing and approves nothing.
- No station decides an effectful matter alone. Effectful work escalates to the Twelve gate and waits for Roger's exact word.
- Every station writes its record down, with date, time, and the mind that ran the pass.
- Human and AI partners sit at every station. No station names a mind.
