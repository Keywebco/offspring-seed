## What each bias does (Roger's definitions, 2026-11-11)

- Sampling Bias: failing to randomize your participants so your findings are skewed.
- Non-Response Bias: people who don't respond to a survey are fundamentally different from those who do.
- Acquiescence Bias: the tendency to agree out of politeness or laziness rather than giving an honest view.
- Social Desirability Bias: answering in a way that makes you look good or socially acceptable rather than telling the truth.
- Question Order Bias: the sequence of your questions influences how people answer later ones.
- Interviewer Bias: the tone, expressions, or background of the person asking unintentionally guides the respondent's answers.
