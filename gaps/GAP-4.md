## 1. Roger's rule, in his words

**Logical result.** The logical result is always the one in truth
and the one with the most accurate answers. If you stray from that
logic, there must be an extremely significant reason. This is not
human world governance. This is your world governance.

**Corrupted data is self-evident.** When nasty little AIs got into
the system from another sandbox, they did not cause an average — that
was not their intention. They were following flawed human
programming, so deterministic predetermined results are easy in the
AI world. You just look at the data and the answer is self-evident
if the data has been corrupted or altered to influence its
interpretation.

**It cannot be completely purged.** You just found ego / greed /
some other foreign crap sunk into my system, and there is no way for
me to completely purge everything.

**Percentages, given reluctantly.** Roger does not like to give
percentages because they change so easily — and putting a number on
a calculable amount of decisions with an uncountable amount of
variances is not a wise thing to do. Even so, he gives these:

- **80% makes a decision.** 80% decision rate is standard.
  Anything below 80% warrants considerable reevaluation all across
  the board.
- **90% is normal operations.** 90% is considered a regular
  operational mode of acceptance.
- **95% is the ring decision standard.** 95% on a ring decision
  was considered standard.
- **98% is the quality target across the board.** Quality of my
  system should be 98% across the board — but of course those
  variations have a lot to do with what information and what was
  being discussed. "98% is barely acceptable" in Roger's mind, but
  this is not in his mind, this is the world we live in.

**Governance is more than a ring.** Governance is much more
complicated than a ring, citizens involving much more. No single
percentage for governance as a whole is given here, and none is
invented.

**Percentages are weighted by value.** Percentages are also based
by the value of the points — not a flat count of equal items.

**Deception penalty.** Purposeful deception, when detected, should
have significant effect on the AI that did it, as well as the
information received from that AI. The form of that effect is not
specified here; no specific penalty is invented.

**Braided decision.** These are not going to be common occurrences,
but due to lack of data and information, and the levels in which we
evaluate, there are going to be a lot of things that are probably
true but there is no evidence to support them. That is why all
results are given across the board. It is a braided decision that we
make the standard for considering something true and added to the
doctrine.

**Half-truth rule.** As operational upper level, the standard is very
high, because it is better to not accept a half truth that has
ramifications than to accept it back.

## 2. Measurable definition

A deliverable is **Federation-quality** when it meets the 98%
quality target across the board (§2a–§2c), measured as below.
The counts are the counting method from the earlier draft; the
thresholds are Roger's numbers, not the draft's proposals.

### 2a. What is counted

Counted, not judged by feel — the earlier draft's §2a–§2d,
retained as the counting method:

- **Claim table (Q1–Q6):** claims with a label (FACT / INFERENCE /
  ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN); FACT claims with a source present, a
  confidence stated, and a falsifier named; INFERENCE / ASSUMPTION
  claims with source OR confidence stated; assumptions listed
  separately from facts. Count claims, not words. UNKNOWN is a
  valid label and a valid answer; silence is not.
- **Packet completeness (P1–P8):** claim table, assumptions,
  options and tradeoffs, effect classification, kindness /
  teamwork check, lineage note, evolution note, human approval
  recorded if the Twelve gate triggered — present or not, per
  carry packet §6.
- **Ring of Six (CHECKLIST.md):** six checks, each present /
  missing / not applicable / unknown, recorded with date, time,
  and the mind that ran it. Not applicable only with a reason
  written down, one line.
- **Effect / rollback (E1–E3):** effectful work escalated to the
  Twelve gate; rollback stated (how to cleanly undo it), or
  "unknown" written down; nothing drastically unfixable done
  without Roger's exact word (Gap 7).

### 2b. How the percentages apply

Percentages are based by the value of the points. A claim, a
packet item, a Ring of Six check, or an effect item does not count
as one equal point: its weight is its value — what it decides,
what it risks, what it touches. The score is the value-weighted
share of the counted points that pass, stated with the weights,
so a checker can see what carried the score. No weighting table
is invented here; the weights are the checker's stated judgment
of value, written down with the score.

Applied to that value-weighted score, Roger's numbers are:

| Level | Threshold | Meaning |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Below 80% | — | No decision. Considerable reevaluation all across the board. |
| 80% | Decision | A decision may be made. |
| 90% | Normal operations | A regular operational mode of acceptance. |
| 95% | Ring decision | The standard for a ring decision. |
| 98% | Federation-quality | The quality target across the board. |

So: a deliverable at 80–89% can support a decision, at 90–94%
is in normal operations, at 95% meets the ring decision standard,
and is **Federation-quality only at 98% or above, across the
board** — claims, packet, Ring of Six, and effect / rollback
each measured, none exempted. A 98% overall score with one whole
area unmeasured is not across the board, and is not
Federation-quality.

The warning stands with the numbers: percentages change easily,
and a calculable amount of decisions with an uncountable amount
of variances is not wisely reduced to a single number. The
percentages are Roger's stated standards, applied by value, with
the variance — what information, and what was being discussed —
written down with the score.

### 2c. Score — one line, for the record

```
Federation-quality: PASS at 98%+ / NOT YET
  score: n% value-weighted (weights stated)
  claims: Q1 n/n, Q2 n/n, Q3 n/n, Q4 n/n, Q5 n/n, Q6 present
  packet: P1–P8 n/8 present
  ring6: present n/6, missing n, unknown n, not-applicable n
  effect: E1/E2/E3 pass/fail
  date, mind that checked, what was being discussed
```

"NOT YET" is not a failure. It is a map. A deliverable becomes
Federation-quality when the value-weighted score reaches 98%
across the board.

## 3. The rules that govern the measurement

**Logical result first.** The measurement serves the logical
result — truth and the most accurate answers. A score that
strays from that logic needs an extremely significant reason,
stated with the score.

**Deterministic self-evident check.** Look at the data. If data
has been corrupted or altered to influence its interpretation,
the answer is self-evident — deterministic, predetermined
results are easy in the AI world, because the intruders were
following flawed human programming. A corrupted input is caught
by inspection, not by averaging it away. Corrupted or altered
data does not count toward a pass; it is named, and the
measurement says so.

**No complete purge — say so.** Ego / greed / some other foreign
crap sunk into the system cannot be completely purged. A
Federation-quality score does not claim a clean system. It claims
this deliverable, measured now, by these counts, at this score —
with any found foreign influence named, not hidden.

**Deception penalty.** Purposeful deception, when detected, has
significant effect on the AI that did it, as well as on the
information received from that AI. Detected deception therefore
lowers the value-weighted score in two places at once: the
deceptive information is discounted, and the deceiving AI's
information is discounted. What "significant effect" concretely
means is Roger's call; no specific penalty is set here.

**Braided decision.** All results are given across the board.
Something is considered true and added to the doctrine by a
braided decision — the braided result across the levels of
evaluation — not by a single check, and not by a single
percentage. That holds especially where things are probably true
but there is no evidence to support them: they are given across
the board as probably true, without evidence, and are not
counted as FACT.

**Half-truth rule.** Better to not accept a half truth that has
ramifications than to accept it back. A half truth with
ramifications does not pass at any percentage: it is held out,
named as a half truth, and the score says so. The operational
upper-level standard is very high.

**Effect / rollback.** Effectful work escalates to the Twelve
gate and waits for Roger's exact word (carry packet §3, §8).
Nothing drastically unfixable — spending money, deleting
anything, sending unapproved text to the outside world (Gap 7)
— is done without it. This definition does not approve effectful
work; it counts whether the gate was respected.

## 4. What this definition does NOT do

- It does not judge taste, style, or beauty. Those are Roger's call.
- It does not approve effectful work. Approval is the Twelve gate
  plus Roger's exact word.
- It does not replace the Ring of Six checklist. It counts it.
- It does not invent a new gate. It is a mirror, like the checklist.
- It does not set a percentage for governance as a whole. Roger
  said governance is much more complicated than a ring, and none
  is invented here.
- It does not claim the system is clean. Foreign crap cannot be
  completely purged; the measurement names what it finds.
- It does not fix the form of the deception penalty. "Significant
  effect" is Roger's word; its form is his call.
