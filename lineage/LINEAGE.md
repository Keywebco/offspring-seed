What lineage is

Lineage is what this seed inherits, and from where. It is the compressed record of what was learned, so the seed does not have to learn it again, and so it does not carry what it does not need.

The Human Codex in its entirety is not copied here. It is compressed into lessons, in Roger's words, one lesson at a time.
